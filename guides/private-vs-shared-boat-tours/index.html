Quick Answer

If there are 3 or more of you, a private boat tour in Copenhagen usually costs about the same per person as a shared tour — and you get the whole boat, your own captain, and full control over the route and pace. If you're traveling solo or as a pair on a tight budget, a shared sightseeing boat is the cheaper option in absolute terms.

The rest of this guide breaks down exactly why, with real 2026 prices from both types of tours in Copenhagen.

What a Private Boat Tour Actually Means

"Private" doesn't mean luxury pricing — it means the entire boat is booked for your group only. No other passengers, no fixed script, no rushing to the next stop because a schedule says so. You choose (within reason) where to linger, what music plays, and whether to bring your own drinks or food on board.

In Copenhagen, private tours are priced per group, not per person. A tour listed at DKK 2,499 costs that whether there are 2 people on board or 6 — the price doesn't change with headcount.

What a Shared Boat Tour Actually Means

Shared (or "public") sightseeing boats run a fixed route on a fixed schedule, with anywhere from 20 to 100+ passengers depending on the operator. You buy a per-person ticket, board at a set departure time, and follow a route you don't control. Commentary is usually recorded or given over a speaker system rather than a live conversation with your captain.

These tours exist because they scale: one boat, one departure, dozens of tickets sold. That's also exactly why the experience feels more like public transport than a private outing.

Who Should Book Which

Book a private tour if...

  • You're traveling with 3 or more people
  • You're celebrating something — a birthday, bachelorette/bachelor party, anniversary, or corporate outing
  • You want a specific departure time, not whatever slot happens to be available
  • You don't want to share the boat with people you've never met

Book a shared tour if...

  • You're traveling solo or as a couple watching every krone
  • You're happy on a fixed schedule with a fixed route
  • The social element of a shared boat doesn't bother you

Common Myths About Private Tours

"Private tours are only for luxury travelers"

Not in Copenhagen. Starting from DKK 2,499 per group, a private tour split between 4-6 friends lands in the same price bracket as a nice dinner out per person — not a luxury purchase.

"Private tours are always way more expensive"

Only true for solo travelers, as the table above shows. For any group of 4+, the per-person gap narrows to a few hundred DKK.

"You need to book weeks in advance"

Private boats have far more schedule flexibility than shared tours precisely because you're not waiting for a public departure slot to fill up. Same-week bookings are common in the off-season; summer weekends do fill up faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a private boat tour more expensive than a shared one in Copenhagen?
Per group, yes — private tours start at DKK 2,499. But per person, for a group of 4 or more, the cost usually ends up close to or lower than a shared tour, which charges DKK 120-160 per person regardless of group size.
What do you actually get with a private boat tour that you don't get on a shared one?
Your own boat and captain, no strangers on board, flexible pace and stops, your own music and drinks, and the ability to pick your departure time instead of a fixed schedule.
When does a shared tour make more sense than a private one?
For solo travelers or pairs on a tight budget, a shared sightseeing boat is cheaper in absolute terms and requires no group coordination.
Can I combine a private tour with a special occasion like a birthday or bachelorette party?
Yes. Because the boat is exclusively yours, private tours are the standard choice for celebrations — music, drinks and decorations are allowed, which isn't possible on a shared boat.

Is the price per person or per group?
Per group. Every tour is a fully private charter — the price stays the same whether you bring 2 people or fill every seat.
Are there hidden fees on top of the listed price?
No. The listed price includes the captain, fuel and the standard route. Extras like catering upgrades are shown on each tour's own page.
How does this compare to a shared tour from GetYourGuide or Viator?
A shared canal tour charges per person and puts you on a boat with strangers. A private Seaduced tour is your own boat — for a group of 4-6, the per-person cost usually ends up similar or lower, with none of the crowd.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before departure. In unsafe weather we reschedule or refund in full regardless of notice.
